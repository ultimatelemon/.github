# Security policy

We take the security of our products and our clients' data seriously. Thank you for helping us keep it that way.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please don't report security issues in public issues, discussions or pull requests.**

Report it privately through GitHub instead:

1. Go to the **Security** tab of the repository.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Describe the issue, how to reproduce it and what impact you expect.

Can't find that button, or is it about something that isn't on GitHub? Reach us through [ultimatelemon.eu](https://ultimatelemon.eu) and ask for a secure way to share the details.

## What happens next

- We confirm that we received your report within 3 working days.
- We keep you posted while we investigate and fix it.
- Once it is fixed, we're happy to credit you, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

Please give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you share it with anyone else, and don't access, change or delete data that isn't yours while testing.
